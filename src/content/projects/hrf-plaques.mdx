## Contributing

Please see our [tagging guidelines](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia/Tags#Historic_Richmond_Church_Hill_Plaques) for mapping Church Hill's plaques in OpenStreetMap. We also suggest that you upload any photographs you may have taken to the [Historic Richmond Church Hill Plaque Program](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Historic_Richmond_Church_Hill_Plaque_Program) category in Wikimedia Commons -- this will make it much easier for them to render on our map!
