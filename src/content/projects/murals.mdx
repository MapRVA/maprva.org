## Contributing

Please see our [MapRoulette Challenge](https://maproulette.org/browse/challenges/54376) or add a mural according to these [tagging guidelines](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Richmond,_Virginia/Tags#Murals) for mapping Richmond's murals in OpenStreetMap. We also suggest that you upload any photographs you may have taken to the [Mapping of Murals in Richmond, Virginia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Mapping_of_Murals_in_Richmond,_Virginia) category in Wikimedia Commons — this will make it much easier for them to render on our map!
